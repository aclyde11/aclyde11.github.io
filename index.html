Research

AI for science, with interpretation kept in the loop.

Scientific AI and HPC

My dissertation work focused on AI and high-performance computing for structure-based drug discovery: accelerating docking, designing scalable screening workflows, and making those workflows useful to domain scientists.

Chemical space and molecular design

Scaffold-based work reframes chemical enumeration around interpretable molecular neighborhoods, graph structure, and design constraints that chemists can reason about.

Interpretability, law, and democracy

My STS and human-rights work asks how model interpretation, evidence, public explanation, and institutional power meet when algorithmic systems shape civic life.
